A Word From Our Chefs

Rensuke Takahashi (Head Chef)

Welcome to our culinary haven! Cooking is not just about mixing ingredients—it’s about crafting experiences, creating memories, and sharing love through flavors. Every dish on our menu is a story, inspired by tradition, passion, and the joy of bringing people together.

Liang Zhenyu (Head Chef)

At RIVA, we believe in using the freshest ingredients, mastering the art of balance, and presenting dishes that not only satisfy the palate but also nourish the soul. Whether it's a comforting classic or a bold new creation, each plate is a reflection of our dedication to excellence.

Alessio Romano (Head Chef)

Cooking is an art, and every plate is a canvas. At RIVA, we pour passion, creativity, and the finest ingredients into every dish to create something truly special. Our philosophy is simple—honor tradition while embracing innovation. Whether it's a timeless favorite or a bold new creation, each dish tells a story, blending flavors that excite the palate and warm the heart.

Amit Saini (Head Chef)

Food is more than just nourishment—it’s an experience, a connection, a moment shared. Every dish at RIVA is crafted with care, inspired by the rich flavors of tradition and the excitement of new possibilities. We believe in fresh, high-quality ingredients, bold yet balanced flavors, and the joy of bringing people together through food. Whether you're here for a comforting classic or an adventurous bite, we hope each meal leaves a lasting impression.
